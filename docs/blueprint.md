# **App Name**: PCMEA Connect

## Core Features:

- News Feed: Aggregated news and announcements from PCMEA and industry sources.
- Events Calendar: Display upcoming industry events, trade shows, and PCMEA meetings.
- Member Directory: Searchable directory of PCMEA members with contact information.
- Resource Library: Central repository for documents, reports, and industry-related materials. The LLM will act as a tool that attempts to intelligently categorize new documents based on keywords, allowing faster user discoverability of the resources
- Personalized Recommendations: AI powered recommendations of relevant news and resources, tailored to each member's profile and interests.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep forest green (#228B22), reflecting the natural materials and craftsmanship in carpet making.
- Background color: Soft, desaturated beige (#F5F5DC), providing a neutral and calming backdrop.
- Accent color: Muted gold (#B8860B) for highlighting important information and CTAs, adding a touch of elegance.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a humanist serif with an elegant, intellectual, contemporary feel; suitable for both headlines and body text.
- Use clean, professional icons with a subtle gold accent to represent different sections and functions.
- Maintain a clean and organized layout with clear visual hierarchy, prioritizing ease of navigation and information accessibility.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being distracting.
